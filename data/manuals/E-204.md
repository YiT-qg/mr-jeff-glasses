# E-204 Spindle overtemp

1. Isolate power at the machine disconnect and apply your lock (LOTO).
2. Verify zero energy at the spindle drive with a meter.
3. Remove the spindle nose cover (4x M6 bolts).
4. Extract bearing 6205-2RS with a puller; inspect the shaft for scoring.
5. Fit new bearing 6205-2RS; refit cover torqued to 12 N-m.
6. Restore power and run a 5-minute warm-up; confirm housing temp below 60 C.
